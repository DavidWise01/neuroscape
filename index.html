MEET YOUR MAINFRAME

You are the pilot. Your cards are your team. Protect your body and your computer, and get either of the rival’s health meters down to zero in this practice game.

01 / INITIALIZE

Collect energy (RAM) or draw a new card. You get one choice on your first turn, then two.

02 / DEPLOY

Put a character on the table. Its RAM stays busy while that character is there.

03 / COMBAT

Choose a ready character to attack. A defender may block. Otherwise the attack hits body or computer health.

04 / RESET

Finish your turn. Surviving characters recover their damage in this trainer; then the bot takes a turn.

Official constructed decks use 50–255 Cyberdeck cards, 25 RAM and one Mainframe. This practice scenario is smaller and is not a legal constructed match.
