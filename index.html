BOOT UP & DIVE IN

Learn the board with authentic Genesis cards and a short, deliberately reduced character-combat scenario.

01 / INITIALIZE

Draw or install RAM. First player gets one action on the opening turn; later turns allow two.

02 / DEPLOY

Deploy a character by committing its RAM cost. That RAM stays occupied until it leaves play.

03 / COMBAT

Declare an attack, let the defender block, or deal damage to its Bioframe or Mainframe.

04 / RESET

Clear character damage. The next turn refreshes running resources and characters.

Official constructed decks use 50–255 Cyberdeck cards, 25 RAM and one Mainframe. This practice scenario is smaller and is not a legal constructed match.
